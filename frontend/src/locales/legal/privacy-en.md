# Privacy Policy — ft-Transcendence (RetroLudo '42)

**Effective date:** 2026-08-26
**Controller:** Team Pace 24, developers of ft-Transcendence (RetroLudo '42) (the "Team")

This Privacy Policy explains how the ft-Transcendence (RetroLudo '42) web application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your personal data. It is prepared in accordance with the **Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act 2010 ("PDPA")** and its guiding principles: General, Notice and Choice, Disclosure, Security, Retention, Data Integrity, and Access.

---

## 1. What personal data we collect

The App collects only the data needed to provide the game and its account features:

| Category | Data | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Account identifiers | username, display name, email address | You provide at registration |
| Authentication | password (stored only as a bcrypt hash — never plaintext) | You provide |
| OAuth identifiers | provider name and provider account ID (Google, GitHub, or 42) | OAuth provider, only if you choose to sign in with it |
| Profile | custom avatar image (if uploaded), default avatar style | You provide |
| Game activity | match history, game results, ratings, win/loss/streak statistics | Generated by your gameplay |
| Social | friends list, friend requests, block lists, game invitations | Generated by your in-app activity |
| Technical | presence/online status, notification preferences | Generated by App use |

We do **not** collect payment card details, location data, or any data from children under the age of 13. The App is a game and does not perform automated profiling beyond displaying game statistics you already see.

---

## 2. Notice and choice (consent)

By creating an account and using the App, you consent to the collection and use of your personal data as described in this Policy. Signing in with an external provider (Google, GitHub, 42) is optional and only occurs when you choose that sign-in method.

- You may withdraw consent by deleting your account at any time (see Section 7).
- Where optional data is involved (e.g., uploading an avatar, linking an OAuth method), it is collected only on your express choice.

---

## 3. How we use your data

We use your personal data solely to:

- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate you (email/password or OAuth)
- Run matches, record results, and maintain leaderboards and statistics
- Manage friends, notifications, and presence status
- Verify your email address and secure your account (two-factor authentication)
- Respond to your requests and provide support

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal data, and we do not use it for marketing or advertising.

---

## 4. Disclosure

Your data is disclosed only as needed to operate the App:

- **Within the App**: game results, usernames, and presence are shown to other players as part of the game (e.g., leaderboards, friend lists, match history). Your **email address and password** are never shown to other players.
- **Service providers**: the App runs on self-hosted infrastructure (Docker containers) and uses third-party OAuth providers (Google, GitHub, 42) and email services solely to deliver the features you use.
- **Legal compliance**: we may disclose data if required to do so by law or by a competent authority.

We do not transfer your personal data outside of the purposes described here without your consent, except where required to provide the service.

---

## 5. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data, including:

- Passwords stored only as **bcrypt hashes** (salted)
- Session tokens stored in **httpOnly** cookies, with short-lived access tokens and revocable refresh tokens
- Two-factor authentication (2FA) available via email codes
- Transport encrypted with **HTTPS/TLS** at the public gateway
- Access to configuration secrets restricted and not committed to source control
- OAuth credentials established via secure provider authorisation flows

Because the App is a self-hosted development/evaluation project, the deployment uses a **self-signed TLS certificate**; the connection is still encrypted, but it is not validated by public certificate authorities. You should not use the App to store highly sensitive data.

---

## 6. Retention

We keep your personal data only for as long as your account exists and as needed to provide the features you use. Ephemeral data (live match state, presence, notifications, temporary security tokens) is held in memory with automatic expiry. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed (see Section 7).

---

## 7. Data integrity, access, and correction (your rights under PDPA)

Under the PDPA you have the right to:

- **Access**: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correction**: update or correct your data (you can also edit most of it yourself in your profile).
- **Withdrawal / deletion**: request deletion of your account and data.

**Deleting your account.** You may delete your account directly in the App (via your profile). Deleting your account:

- Permanently removes your profile, match history, achievements, friends, notifications, and uploaded avatar;
- Removes your data from the leaderboard; and
- Logs you out of all devices.

For any access, correction, or other request, contact the Team (see Section 9).

---

## 8. Data integrity

We take reasonable steps to ensure the personal data we hold is accurate, complete, and not misleading, and we correct or update it when you notify us or edit it yourself.
